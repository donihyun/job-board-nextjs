# Design — VIKB

A locked design system for this app. Every page should use the same visual language.

## Genre
Modern-minimal, with a utilitarian job-search tone.

## Macrostructure family
- Marketing pages: Workbench — show the real search flow before marketing copy.
- App pages: Workbench — filters, results, and actions carry the interface.
- Content pages: Long Document — readable country and visa guidance.

## Theme
- Paper: cool near-white
- Ink: blue-black
- Accent: electric cobalt, limited to actions and focus
- Rules: visible cool-grey borders

## Typography
- Display: Space Grotesk 600, roman
- Body: Noto Sans 400–600
- Display tracking: -0.025em

## Spacing
Use the named 4-point scale in `tokens.css`.

## Motion
Minimal. Transform and opacity only. Reduced motion disables spatial movement.

## CTA voice
- Primary: solid cobalt, 6px radius, direct verb-led copy.
- Secondary: plain text or bordered white control.

## What pages MUST share
- VIKB wordmark, cobalt accent, type pairing, focus treatment, and compact controls.
- Factual copy only. No invented metrics, testimonials, or service promises.

## Per-page allowances
- Marketing: one real travel image is allowed.
- App: no decorative enrichment.
- Content: typography and rules only.

## Archetypes
- Navigation: N9 Edge-aligned minimal.
- Footer: Ft2 Inline single line.
